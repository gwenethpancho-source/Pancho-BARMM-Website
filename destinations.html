01

Lake Lanao

The largest lake in Mindanao and the center of Maranao life. Its shores are home to rich traditions in weaving, brasswork and okir wood carving.

Location: Marawi City and Lanao del Sur

02 / 05

02

Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah Masjid

Also called the Grand Mosque, it is one of the largest mosques in the country. Its golden dome and tall minarets stand out in the city skyline.

Location: Cotabato City

03 / 05

03

Sheikh Karimul Makhdum Mosque

Built in 1380, this is considered the oldest mosque in the Philippines. It marks where Islam first arrived in the islands.

Location: Simunul, Tawi-Tawi

04 / 05

04

Sitangkai

Called the “Venice of the South” because houses stand on stilts above the sea and boats serve as transport between them.

Location: Sitangkai, Tawi-Tawi

05 / 05

05

Bud Bongao

A mountain above Bongao town with a long trail that is visited by locals and travelers. Wild monkeys live along the path, so keep your food covered.

Location: Bongao, Tawi-Tawi
