Before you go

Travel tips

  • 01

    Check government travel advisories and ask local tourism offices for guidance before visiting any area.

  • 02

    Hire local, accredited guides and arrange transport in advance.

  • 03

    Dress modestly, covering shoulders and knees. Women may be asked to cover their hair inside mosques.

  • 04

    Ask before taking photos of people or prayer areas.

  • 05

    Plan around prayer times and Ramadan, since some shops and restaurants change their hours.

  • 06

    Bring cash, since many places have few ATMs.
