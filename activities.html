Four ways to experience the Bangsamoro

Activities & Experiences

Nature & Adventure
01

Nature and Adventure

  • Take a boat ride across Lake Lanao
  • Climb Bud Bongao in Tawi-Tawi
  • Visit island communities in Tawi-Tawi with local guides
Culture & Heritage
02

Culture and Heritage

  • Visit the Grand Mosque in Cotabato City
  • See the Sheikh Karimul Makhdum Mosque in Simunul
  • Learn about Maranao and Maguindanao history from local guides
Island Life
03

Island Life

  • See the stilt houses of Sitangkai
  • Take a boat between islands
  • Watch fishers and seaweed farmers at work
Food & Crafts
04

Food and Crafts

  • Eat piaparan, a chicken dish with coconut and palapa
  • Try pastil, rice with shredded chicken in banana leaf
  • Shop for inaul, Yakan and Maranao woven cloth and brassware
